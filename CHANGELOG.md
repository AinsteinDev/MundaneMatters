# Changelog

## [0.2.0] - unreleased (work in progress)

First public build.

### Added
- **Trade, inn rooms and training** from natural speech, with real prices, haggling and "on the house".
- **Promises:** NPCs hand over the items, gold and beds they promise, now or later, and can cancel them.
- **Generosity budget** per NPC, based on relationship (food counts half).
- **Guest rights:** "you're always welcome" gives you a bed in their home on every visit.
- **Permission to take** objects in NPC homes ("go ahead, take the wine").
- **Shared meals** with friends: they sit at a table and share a dish.
- **Gifts** in your own words, with an inventory picker for "I brought you something", and a rare, capped relationship gain.
- **Follow and escort** through SeverActions: home, "someone's place", kinds of place ("the brewery"), local-only trips.
- **Accept/Decline popups** whose buttons are your character's lines.
- **Bio decorator:** NPCs know their real prices and what they owe you.
- **In-game self-test** for your classifier model.
